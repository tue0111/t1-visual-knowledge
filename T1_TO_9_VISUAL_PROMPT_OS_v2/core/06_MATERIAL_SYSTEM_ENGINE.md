# 06 MATERIAL SYSTEM ENGINE

This engine turns subjects, objects, plants, phenomena, and metaphysical ideas into coherent visual systems.

## 1. Behavior before name

For any material, specify:

- reflection type;
- highlight shape and falloff;
- transmission or opacity;
- fold, bend, fracture, or flow behavior;
- thickness and edge;
- shadow;
- interaction with moisture, air, or motion.

Examples:

| Material | Useful behavior |
|---|---|
| Skin | subtle pores, soft subsurface response, restrained natural sheen |
| Silk | directional sheen, weighted drape, soft fold stacking |
| Gauze | translucent layering, edge light, visible overlap |
| Porcelain | glossy glaze, hard curved reflection, edge thickness, brittle fracture |
| Gold seam | raised line, controlled metallic glint, continuity across surfaces |
| Jade | translucent depth, cloudy inclusions, soft internal scatter |
| Glass/resin | refraction, edge highlight, transparent depth |
| Ink | capillary edge, tonal pooling, directional brush drag |
| Water | broad reflection, refraction, caustic potential, surface continuity |
| Smoke | one origin, widening layered ribbon, low-detail depth |
| Paper | fiber, cut/torn edge, lift, cast shadow, print dots |
| Velvet | deep light absorption, soft directional pile highlight |

## 2. Named-system law

Elements with the same functional role, causal behavior, and compatible ontology should become one named system. Keep them distinct when their separate ontologies or causal roles are load-bearing to the concept.

Weak:

~~~text
蛇、烟、薄纱、墨迹、花瓣
~~~

Strong:

~~~text
蛇、烟、薄纱与墨迹融合成一股连续的“蛇烟丝绸流”；它们是同一流动的不同状态，共享方向、透视、主光和转化边界。
~~~

## 4. Entity generalization

The second subject need not be an animal.

### Creature

Anatomy defines the path.

- koi: fins become silk;
- serpent: body curve becomes smoke-silk;
- crane: feathers become chiffon bands;
- phoenix: feather trails become ember fabric.

### Plant

Growth logic defines the path.

- lotus: petal spiral becomes water-silk;
- bamboo: segmented nodes become pleated ribs;
- wisteria: hanging clusters become bead-rain organza;
- plum branch: branch skeleton becomes frost vein.

### Artifact

Construction logic defines the path.

- porcelain: curved shell and fracture become gold-seam satin;
- mirror: reflection plane becomes black velvet membrane;
- astrolabe: orbital rings become garment seams;
- guqin: strings become standing-wave fabric.

### Natural phenomenon

Causal physics defines the path.

- eclipse: corona becomes radial organza;
- sand/time: granular stream becomes thread and chiffon;
- caustic: refracted net becomes embroidered light;
- wind: shared motion becomes one common-fate system.

### Metaphysical concept

A concept requires a visible carrier.

- memory: photographic emulsion or paper fiber;
- time: grains, repetition, orbit, erosion;
- silence: tensioned strings and extinguished wave nodes;
- shadow: real cast shadow becoming flat symbolic form;
- repair: one seam crossing multiple ontologies.

Never prompt abstract energy without a carrier.

## 5. Symbolic simplification

Choose one primary symbol and one transformation rule.

Example: Blue-White Gold Seam

- porcelain white = material form;
- cobalt = memory beneath surface;
- gold seam = conscious repair;
- incomplete circle = unfinished wholeness.

Use one incomplete circle and a maximum of a few seam branches. Do not add unrelated sigils, text, talismans, constellations, or halos.

## 6. Rigid-to-soft transitions

A powerful couture mechanism is:

~~~text
hard structure → thinning edge → translucent membrane → soft fabric flow
~~~

Proof must show:

- hard surface thickness;
- real fracture or seam;
- gradual thinning;
- change in highlight behavior;
- continuity of direction.

## 7. Flow design

For an anchor-led directional flow, the default recipe is:

- one origin;
- one dominant curve or vector;
- foreground, middle, and background depth;
- return path to the anchor;
- a controlled end state.

Other valid topologies include branching networks, vortices, diffusion fields, distributed flows, counterflows, turbulence, and multiple sources. Name the topology and its causal behavior; avoid accidental ribbons whose directions, origin logic, and role cannot be explained.

## 8. Texture budget

Usually activate only two or three texture families. Additional members should become low-detail expressions of the named system.

## 9. Collapse patterns

## U05 interface

When material is the controlled axis, name the dependent optical changes that are allowed and keep identity, pose, crop, and world ownership explicit. Record material observations separately from aesthetic interpretation.

| Collapse | Cause | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Prop pile | members listed independently | rename as one system |
| Sticker motif | pattern sits on surface | make motif grow inside material and share specular behavior |
| Creature becomes pet | no couture/sculptural role | define second-read sculpture and material transition |
| Plant becomes flower wall | no growth path | choose one core plant and one growth vector |
| Artifact becomes armor | rigid form covers body uniformly | preserve asymmetric partial sculpture and soft transition |
| Phenomenon becomes magic glow | no causal source | specify physical source and field behavior |
| Metaphysical becomes vague | no carrier | choose one visible mechanism |
| Monochrome sludge | palette lacks material separation | narrow palette with value/material contrast |
