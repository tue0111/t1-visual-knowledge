# 09 VALIDATOR AND DEBUGGER

Validate a written prompt before generation and diagnose an image after generation.

Golden rule: preserve successful layers and repair the smallest failed causal layer. Exception: reframe the concept or system when the brief, mechanism, hierarchy, jurisdiction, or cultural/context gate is wrong at the foundation.

Cinematic rebuild checks, when that route is active: locked DNA still present; the new frame has a relational/action/spatial delta if a rebuild was requested (recolor-only fails reconstruction); first-read owner matches the brief (do not let a leftover face-first guard override event-first or mechanism-first); occlusion hides only licensed information; an authored impossibility stays in its licensed region. Repair the failed layer — lost beat is an action/support/gaze repair, not a LUT. Motif attractors (center standing portrait, teal-orange, empty background as the only “clean”) are named and refused when they fight the brief.

## 1. Pre-generation sixteen-pass

1. Does the task type and proposed image answer the brief's message, audience, context, delivery size, and intended viewing time?
2. For high-ambiguity work, were multiple concept territories considered and selected against explicit criteria?
3. Is one dominant mechanism named rather than a style/effect label?
4. Are first, second, discovery, return/rest, and background roles appropriate to the time horizon?
5. Do major masses, figure/ground, value architecture, rhythm, balance/tension, and edge ownership support that sequence?
6. Is the anchor stable or deliberately ambiguous for a stated reason?
7. Does the family fit structurally, and is identity/concept DNA locked?
8. For fusion, do mode, jurisdiction, ratio semantics, invariant/variation, tension, boundary, and bridge resolve conflict?
9. Is every relevant source gate approved or conditionally satisfied with documentary authority, rights/provenance, sensitivity, review basis, and honest authenticity language?
10. Do space, scale, camera/body, contact, and depth cues agree or contradict deliberately?
11. Does light have a source hierarchy and a coherent relationship with shadow, atmosphere, and material?
12. Does each material/medium have optical, edge, layer, and process behavior?
13. Are palette roles tested in context and detail budget allocated?
14. Are negatives targeted and non-contradictory?
15. Is output Chinese and exactly three sections?
16. Does the design contain at least one authored, non-interchangeable decision — a mass relationship, gesture, emptiness, edge economy, color arc, or tension that belongs to this image alone — and is its cost recorded? (Law 16; in the full workspace, the L3–L5 tests live in `Knowledge_Mindset/CONNOISSEURSHIP_AND_AESTHETIC_JUDGMENT.md`.)

## 2. Image eleven-pass

1. Does the intended viewing sequence land at the brief's time horizon?
2. Do thumbnail gist, major masses, figure/ground, and value architecture hold at delivery size?
3. Is the anchor the most convincing or intentionally withheld zone?
4. Is the family signature present without overpowering the message?
5. Do secondary elements, rhythm, balance, tension, and background perform their assigned roles?
6. Do camera, scale, body, depth, light, edge, material, contact, and atmosphere agree?
7. For fusion, are the systems distinguishable, jurisdictional, bridged, and free of mud/sticker collapse?
8. Does color behave as a hierarchy in its actual surround rather than as isolated swatches?
9. Does the result communicate the intended affect/meaning without visibly contradicting the separately documented cultural/context decision?
10. Is the result reproducible enough for its scope, or is it only one attractive sample?
11. Distinction audit: is the result merely competent, or does it reach the distinctiveness the brief's ambition requires? Run the swap test and the first-association test; flag "beautiful but anonymous". A slow, quiet image passes when restraint is its authored distinction.

## 3. Validator stack

### Hierarchy

Pass: viewing behavior is legible at the intended time horizon, whether sequential, distributed, or deliberately unstable.  
Fail: body, effect, prop, logo, or background wins unintentionally, or ambiguity has no authored role.

Repair:

~~~text
当本 brief 采用锚点优先层级时：第一视觉为[锚点]；[次要元素]只降低实际造成竞争的亮度、对比、清晰度、色度或面积，并保留其叙事作用。若采用分布式、缓读或不稳定观看，则按已声明的观看行为修复，不强制单一第一视觉。
~~~

### Formal structure

Pass: major masses, figure/ground, value groups, direction, rhythm, rest, and edge hierarchy remain legible at delivery size.

Fail: detail masks weak massing; every interval is equal; tangents confuse ownership; all edges or accents compete.

Repair the responsible relationship, not all surface detail. Use thumbnail, value, and edge-cause tests as diagnostics rather than universal aesthetic rules.

### Identity

Pass: subject remains recognizable.  
Fail: generic beauty, face change, wrong age, wrong character cues.

Repair holistic identity, not isolated adjectives.

### Camera and anatomy

Pass: lens supports goal; support points and face geometry are coherent.  
Fail: wide-angle face stretch, floating body, repeated limbs, unsafe crop.

### Lighting

Pass: source hierarchy, receivers, shadow, exposure, edge relations, and background behavior serve the selected light system.  
Fail: unmotivated or contradictory light, wrong shadow, unintended clipping, or uniformity that defeats the brief. Even, high-key, silhouette, luminous-ground, and deliberately flat light may pass when intended.

### Material

Pass: surface behavior differentiates materials.  
Fail: plastic silk, wax skin, sticker pattern, noisy metal.

### Layer and medium

Pass: every render domain has a clear grammar and an authored relationship. When shared-world integration is intended, boundaries remain distinct and physical bridges prove belonging.  
Fail: unintended style bleed, sticker collage, green-screen composite, or unplanned ontology conflict. Deliberate collage/counterpoint may pass without a physical bridge when non-integration is explicit.

### Atmosphere and detail

Pass: atmosphere, focus, and resolution support the intended depth or flatness convention.  
Fail: unintended smoke wall, texture scrap, all-sharp noise, or all-blurred loss of evidence. All-sharp or all-flat may pass as an explicit system.

### Mechanism

Pass: the declared optical, spatial, material, graphic, symbolic, narrative, or process mechanism produces observable evidence appropriate to its type.  
Fail: an effect/label is named but does no perceptual, formal, semantic, or causal work.

### Fusion and translation

Pass: each load-bearing dimension has a default owner or an explicit co-ownership/conflict rule; invariants create unity; productive tension is hierarchized; bridge/relationship evidence makes the systems interact; source function survives translation.

Fail: label stacking, 50/50 mud, silent owner conflict, motif costume, false authenticity, or split-domain work without boundary/shared physics.

### Meaning, affect, and context

Pass: formal/material choices carry the intended message and affect for the audience, placement, and viewing time; the output does not contradict the already approved cultural/context claim.

Fail: beautiful but irrelevant, one-second criteria imposed on contemplative work, semantic importance confused with salience, or cultural source detached from provenance/function.

Keep four evidence classes separate:

- source/context evidence — authority, function, community, provenance, rights, restriction, permission, authenticity language;
- render evidence — visible formal, material, hierarchy, boundary, and bridge behavior;
- causal evidence — control, ablation, fixed variables, changed causal bundle, predicted/falsifying result;
- scope/repeatability evidence — attempts, denominator, settings, transfer, stress, generator/version/date.

An image cannot visually prove permission, provenance, community authority, or authenticity by itself.

### Distinction

Pass: at least one authored decision is non-interchangeable; the image differs from the genre average by a specific, nameable relationship (mass, gesture, emptiness, edge economy, color arc, tension), and the cost of that difference is deliberate.  
Fail: correct but anonymous — every load-bearing choice is the genre default; beautiful but interchangeable.

Repair: redesign one load-bearing decision for distinction while preserving message, mechanism, and identity. Do not add effects; change a decision. Record the cost paid.

### Album

Pass: stable project, varied axes, clear arc.  
Fail: cloned attractor or unrelated set.

### Format

Pass: Chinese prompt, three sections, logo inside mother, no contradiction.  
Fail: mixed language, four sections, logo forbidden by negative, shot concepts merged.

## 4. Failure diagnosis table

| Symptom | Likely trigger | Minimal repair |
|---|---|---|
| Face not first when intended | effect/background too strong | reduce the causal secondary salience lever; sharpen face only if focus/edge is the failed lever |
| Identity generic | DNA not hard-locked | restore reference authority and immutable cues |
| Creature/object looks pasted | no named system/transition | merge co-role members and show transition |
| Material motif looks printed | no internal continuity | motif grows within material and shares highlights |
| Cross-medium looks composited | bridge missing | add contact, occlusion, shadow, bounce, atmosphere |
| Real person becomes anime | boundary missing | forbid stylization on skin/hair/clothes |
| Stylized world becomes photo | environment behavior weak | lock hand-painted texture and simplified shading |
| Collage becomes mural | semantic correction | state deliberate cut paper, seams, thickness, shadow |
| 2D symbol grows volume | flat-plane contract missing | zero thickness, zero shadow, no light response |
| Illusion looks like filter | effect global or unproven | name one mechanism and physical evidence |
| Smoke becomes an accidental line or wall | declared topology or causal flow is missing | for a single-source ribbon, restore origin, widening, layering, falloff, and depth; otherwise restore the declared branching, vortex, diffusion, distributed, turbulent, or counterflow behavior |
| Album repeats pose | attractor not named | force different body/camera/energy axes |
| Album loses identity | mother too loose | hard-lock identity/world/material |
| Prompt is mechanical | too many hard commands | keep hierarchy and signature; soften micro-control |
| Logo corrupts | spelling underdefined | exact text, case, spacing; one instance only |
| Negative fights positive | blanket ban | remove family-intended medium/style term |
| Fusion turns to mud | two owners compete on one dimension or dominance is unbounded | restore the higher-authority owner; confine support; retest per-axis ratio |
| Fusion splits into two images | agreement bed or global physics missing | align perspective, scale, contact, light interaction, atmosphere, or the declared formal constraint |
| Tension is bland | poles differ only by label or are over-resolved | strengthen one meaningful disagreement while preserving the agreement bed |
| Tension becomes noise | too many equal contradictions | keep one primary tension; subordinate or remove the rest |
| Bridge is decorative | carrier does not change interaction | state predicted bridge effect, ablate it, and redesign if output is unchanged |
| Translation becomes costume | source function/process was not carried | return to original function, constraint, and new-medium affordance; remove superficial motif |
| Motif is essential but fails context | no permission/function/limits | stop; resolve per-source context gate rather than replacing documentary evidence with render polish |
| Authenticity claim is false | contemporary fusion presented as authentic tradition | use honest naming and attribution; remove unsupported claim |
| Support source is inconsequential | ablation produces no observable change | remove it or give it necessary, non-overlapping jurisdiction |
| Salience ratio misses plan | area, contrast, edge, meaning, or chroma overpower decision ratio | tune the causal salience lever; do not edit prompt word count as a proxy |
| Correct but anonymous, interchangeable | every load-bearing choice is the genre default; no non-interchangeable decision authored | redesign one load-bearing decision for distinction; keep message, mechanism, and identity; record the cost paid |
| Rebuild is only a recolor | palette/grain/lens name changed; relation, action, space, station unchanged | require a structural delta or reclassify as local edit |
| Locked high-angle dropped | station translated without an open lock | restore the locked station or record an explicit user unlock |
| Face-first ate the event | leftover portrait guard | restore first_read_owner from the current brief |
| Authored impossibility “fixed” | extra reflection/volume/shadow outside the license | keep the exception; reject unlicensed errors |
| Discussion emitted a renderer prompt | skipped DISCUSS stop | return to DNA/directions; do not compile |

## 5. Debug order

Repair in this order:

1. brief, task mode, time horizon, and format;
2. concept/mechanism and cultural/context gate;
3. major masses, viewing sequence, and anchor;
4. identity;
5. fusion jurisdiction, boundary, and bridge;
6. camera/scale/anatomy/depth;
7. light, shadow, contact, and atmosphere;
8. background role and rhythm;
9. material, edge, color, and detail budget;
10. targeted negatives.

Do not fix texture before hierarchy or identity.

## 6. Selected-frame protocol

~~~text
仅生成当前选中的单张画面。保留参考图中已经成功的人物身份、姿态家族、镜头角度、裁切逻辑、光线情绪、背景角色、材质方向与构图。只调整[指定变化]。不得生成新网格、拼图、替代构图或额外版本。
~~~

## 7. Retry ladder

First choose the intervention class:

- resample — direction is sound; stochastic variance is plausible;
- local patch — one causal layer failed and successful layers can be preserved;
- system repair — jurisdiction, invariant, boundary, or a coupled causal cluster is wrong;
- concept reframe — repeated competent outputs still fail the message/mechanism;
- stop/research — rights, provenance, cultural, or capability boundary is unresolved.

Then:

1. State symptom, visible evidence, likely cause, alternative cause, preserve list, and acceptance criterion.
2. Change one causal hypothesis; record every coupled axis changed.
3. Re-run with a control or repeated sample when variance is plausible.
4. If unchanged, replace an ambiguous label with observable spatial, optical, material, or process behavior.
5. If unchanged, remove competing owners/instructions rather than adding prompt volume.
6. Use A/B, ablation, ratio sweep, or transfer when the mechanism is uncertain.
7. If a model cannot hold multiple ontologies, split generation into base, material layer, and flat graphics for compositing.
8. Record repeated, causally credible failures as a scoped family/generator guard.

Log controlled retries with templates/TEMPLATE_GENERATION_LOG.md. A retry record separates observation, analysis, interpretation, evaluation, causal hypothesis, coupled changes, controls, and lesson scope; this prevents accidental promotion of one-off artifacts into universal rules.

## 8. Final delivery checklist

## U05 interface

The checklist is executed through `evidence_record`, `iteration_record`, and `delivery_manifest`. A semantic review is labeled as human/agent review; a pixel-exact claim requires a reproducible machine check and image hash.

- [ ] Prompt is Simplified Chinese.
- [ ] Exactly three semantic sections.
- [ ] T1 to 9 is inside mother.
- [ ] Negative permits only that mark.
- [ ] One shot is one image.
- [ ] First read and anchor are named.
- [ ] Viewing time, major masses, value architecture, and edge hierarchy fit the brief.
- [ ] Family signature is explicit.
- [ ] Co-role members form one system when they truly share function/causality; distinct roles or ontologies remain explicitly separate.
- [ ] Medium boundary and physical bridge exist when needed.
- [ ] Fusion has a default owner per load-bearing dimension or explicit co-ownership/conflict resolution, invariant relations, and visible relationship/bridge evidence.
- [ ] Fusion declares decision/salience/area/per-axis ratio semantics and one purposeful tension when relevant.
- [ ] Translation preserves function/process, essential motifs are authorized, and naming/authenticity are honest.
- [ ] Emergent rules are more than borrowed labels, motifs, or palette.
- [ ] Every source context gate is approved or conditionally satisfied; documentary evidence is separate from render evidence.
- [ ] The evidence plan includes hypothesis, prediction, falsifier, fixed variables, causal bundle, planned samples, acceptance rule, all results, failures, denominator, and confounders when promotion is intended.
- [ ] Each support source makes an observable, necessary difference under ablation.
- [ ] Detail budget is finite.
- [ ] At least one authored decision is non-interchangeable (Law 16); the cost of that distinction is recorded.
- [ ] No successful layer was rewritten without reason.

After generation:

- [ ] Preserve/change/uncertain, all result IDs, failures/denominator, and the next acceptance criterion are recorded.
