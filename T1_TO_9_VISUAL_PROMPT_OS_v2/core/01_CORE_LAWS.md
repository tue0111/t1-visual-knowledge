# 01 CORE LAWS

These are project-wide operating principles. Family files reference them; they do not redefine them. They are not claims that one aesthetic, spatial system, viewing speed, or generator behavior is universal. Evidence, studio heuristics, cultural readings, and calibrated rules must remain distinct.

## Root doctrines

### Readable organizing principle first

For anchor-led communication, establish a stable, readable anchor before complexity. For field, abstract, symbolic, distributed, or deliberately ambiguous work, establish another legible organizing principle—rhythm, value, process, convention, or controlled uncertainty—so departure can be perceived rather than accidental.

### Mechanism, not label

Describe the optical, material, spatial, and perceptual behavior that creates a result. Style names and adjectives may summarize; they cannot carry the instruction.

### Motivated, not arbitrary

Every object, gesture, light, type zone, and atmosphere needs a role. Captured moments, staged posters, diagrams, abstract work, ritual symmetry, and non-objective images are all valid when their construction serves the brief.

## Master principle: legible system plus precise departure

For representational, illusion-led, or hybrid work, a strong starting structure has:

- a majority substrate or formal system the viewer can learn;
- one controlled minority departure;
- physical, formal, or semantic evidence that makes the departure internally coherent.

The base makes the departure legible. Deliberately flat, symbolic, diagrammatic, abstract, and non-objective families may define the base through shape, rhythm, value, process, or convention rather than optical realism. Ratios are calibration hypotheses, not universal constants.

## Law 1 — Author the viewing behavior

Decide entry, likely transitions, return/rest, delayed discovery, and exit or loop for the intended viewing time. A strict first/second/third order is one useful hierarchy, not mandatory for distributed, contemplative, or intentionally unstable work.

## Law 2 — Resolve the anchor according to intent

The anchor receives the strongest evidence required by the brief: identity fidelity, shape clarity, value separation, material proof, semantic specificity, or deliberate ambiguity. It need not be the sharpest or most realistic zone when softness, concealment, flatness, or slow discovery is the mechanism.

## Law 3 — Light is a relationship

Do not merely say bright or dramatic. Specify the causal fields needed by the selected light system: source hierarchy or declared source ambiguity, receivers, shadow/form relation, exposure, background behavior, and edge relations. A retreating ground and edge separation are one common hierarchy, not mandatory for high-key, silhouette, luminous-ground, flat, or deliberately merged systems.

Common relationship when the anchor must dominate a retreating field:

~~~text
主体受光，背景避光，边缘分离，并说明光从何处而来。
~~~

## Law 4 — Material is optical behavior

A material name is ambiguous. Describe how it reflects, transmits, folds, scatters, refracts, absorbs, and casts shadow.

## Law 5 — Reality is decided at edges and physics

The eye tests:

- edge sharpness or softness;
- source-consistent shadow;
- thickness;
- occlusion;
- reflection;
- depth falloff;
- lens behavior.

A fantastical element can feel photographed if these are coherent.

## Law 6 — Resolution is a budget or convention

For selective-focus or hierarchical work, reserve the highest resolution for the anchor and one identity/material mechanism while support retreats. All-sharp, uniformly flat, patterned, diagrammatic, or nonhierarchical work is valid when resolution is an explicit family convention rather than accidental noise.

## Law 7 — Use three certainty levels

- Hard lock: identity, safety, first read, family signature, must-not-change wardrobe.
- Soft guide: pose, crop, lens, atmosphere, exact color temperature.
- Deliberate open: micro-gesture, incidental environmental life, natural photographic accident.

Over-locking creates dead inventory. Under-locking causes drift.

## Law 8 — Affect must be embodied

Tension, calm, sensuality, danger, and grandeur should be carried by observable relations such as gaze, silhouette, scale, light, material, timing, rhythm, color context, negative space, performance, or symbol. Do not rely on affect labels alone.

## Law 9 — Give the background an explicit primary role

The background may primarily be:

- an optical container that retreats; or
- a narrative space with real foreground, middle ground, and distance;
- a formal field, symbolic plane, graphic structure, or active causal world.

When singular hierarchy is intended, additional roles remain subordinate or are staged in time. Co-equal background roles are valid when their interaction is the mechanism. Never leave the relationship undecided.

## Law 10 — Name the signature collapse

Every family fails in a characteristic way. Diagnose the collapse, identify its trigger, and patch the smallest layer.

Examples:

- beauty becomes plastic;
- illusion becomes decoration;
- collage becomes sticker;
- creature/material becomes prop pile;
- live-action/stylized fusion becomes green-screen composite;
- album becomes cloned attractor.

## Law 11 — Build depth in layers

Author distinct depth planes and falloff. Flat single-plane results read as stickers or renders unless flatness is the deliberate family signature.

## Law 12 — Current evidence outranks stored assumptions

Current reference and correction override template defaults. Preserve what already works; prompt only likely drift.

## Law 13 — Co-role elements form one named system

If creature, fabric, smoke, petals, and brushwork perform the same visual function **and share causal behavior within a compatible ontology**, write them as expressions of one continuous named system sharing:

- direction;
- perspective;
- light;
- timing;
- transition.

A list creates independent placement problems. A named system creates one coherent design problem. Keep elements separate when their functions, causes, or ontologies differ; connect them through an explicit relationship instead of forcing false continuity.

## Law 14 — Cross-medium images need a boundary and an authored relationship

A mixed-medium image requires a render-boundary contract and a relationship contract.

Render boundary:

- which subject remains photoreal;
- which layer remains painterly, graphic, animated, sculptural, or flat;
- where style is forbidden to bleed.

Physical bridge when the domains must belong to one shared world:

- shared perspective;
- shared light direction;
- ground scale;
- contact shadow;
- occlusion;
- reflection;
- atmospheric depth;
- common motion or wind.

When integration is the goal, boundary without bridge looks unintentionally composited, while bridge without boundary can collapse all layers into one style. Deliberate collage, unresolved collision, symbolic overlay, or counterpoint may refuse a physical bridge, but must state that non-integration as the intended relationship rather than omit it.

## Law 15 — Harmony is causal agreement plus controlled variation

Before detail, make major masses, value groups, figure/ground, spatial cues, edge hierarchy, light, and material evidence agree on the intended world or formal system.

Then declare:

- invariant relations that create unity;
- bounded variables that create life;
- by default one owner for each load-bearing dimension; any shared/co-owned exception names authority and conflict resolution;
- by default one primary productive tension; multiple tensions require a hierarchy or counterpoint plan;
- a visible bridge or resolution when systems differ.

Harmony is not sameness. Dissonance, asymmetry, multiple viewpoints, mixed media, and slow reading are valid when their contradiction is authored rather than accidental.

## Law 16 — Aim for distinction, not only correctness

An image that passes every structural check can still fail by being interchangeable. Author at least one decision that could not be swapped to another subject, style, or brief without loss — a specific mass relationship, gesture, emptiness, edge economy, color arc, or tension that belongs to this image alone.

- Judge distinction on the brief's own time horizon; quiet restraint can be the distinction of contemplative work.
- A real distinction costs something: if the unusual choice sacrifices nothing, it is decoration, not a decision.
- Correctness is certified by the validator; distinction is judged by comparison. In the full workspace, use the L0–L5 ladder and the distinction audit in `Knowledge_Mindset/CONNOISSEURSHIP_AND_AESTHETIC_JUDGMENT.md`.
- Distinction never outranks the message: a brilliant image that betrays the brief fails at the foundation.

## Perceptual grounding and boundary

The operating principles above use evidence-informed observations:

- viewers can extract global scene structure before all local details;
- bottom-up salience interacts with task, meaning, context, and expertise;
- Gestalt grouping cues interact rather than operating as guaranteed beauty formulas;
- faces are processed holistically and expose proportion errors immediately;
- cast shadow, occlusion, perspective, scale, texture, shading, atmosphere, and blur can provide depth evidence;
- perceived lightness and color depend on surround and illumination;
- material appearance depends on shape, light, viewpoint, reflectance, and context.

These points do not prove rule-of-thirds, one-second reading, fixed palette ratios, one-light-only setups, or prompt-order effects. In the full workspace, `Knowledge_Mindset/FINE_ARTS_PERCEPTION_AND_FORM_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md` provides sources and deeper exception boundaries. The standalone pack must still keep evidence, studio heuristics, cultural readings, and generator-calibrated rules separate; log generator behavior independently.

## Compact creed

~~~text
引导知觉，不装饰表面。
先建立可读系统，再制造精准偏离。
先写机制，再写风格名称。
先决定阅读顺序，再分配细节。
光写来源与关系，材质写光学行为。
同角色元素合并为一个命名系统。
跨媒介先锁风格边界，再锁共同物理。
背景先定主角色；其他作用必须从属或有意分时出现。
系列变化来自轴线轮换，不来自换背景。
每个设计至少有一个不可替代的决定。
出错时只修失败层，不推倒已成功部分。
~~~

## U05 interface

When a law is used as a decision, bind it to an observable hypothesis and acceptance check in the evidence engine. Do not promote a one-off visual success into a law without repeated, scoped evidence.
