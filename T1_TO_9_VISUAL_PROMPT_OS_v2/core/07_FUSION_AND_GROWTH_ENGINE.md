# 07 FUSION AND GROWTH ENGINE

Use this engine when no family fits or when two or more visual systems must create a controlled synthesis. One support system is the default. More are permitted only when each has non-overlapping, necessary jurisdiction and a structural justification. In the full workspace, deep formation guidance lives in `Knowledge_Mindset/ART_FUSION_AND_CREATIVE_SYNTHESIS.md`; this file remains independently executable when that companion is absent.

## 1. Select, birth, or fuse

Select when the required anchor, viewing sequence, spatial grammar, light/material behavior, and signature move already exist.

Birth when no registered family can answer the brief without changing its structure.

Fuse when two or more systems must interact through explicit ownership, invariant relations, tension, and bridge. A list of style labels, borrowed motifs, or colors is not fusion.

## 2. Context gate before extraction

When a source belongs to a tradition, community, historical artifact, living artist, sacred/ceremonial context, or restricted collection, record:

~~~yaml
context_gate:
  source_named_precisely:
  community_region_period:
  original_function:
  authoritative_source:
  rights_and_provenance:
  status: unknown | green | amber | red
  sensitivity_flags:
  allowed_extraction:
  essential_authorized_motifs:
  forbidden_extraction:
  authenticity_claim:
  review_state: incomplete | conditional | approved | rejected
  review_basis:
  reviewed_by:
  conditions:
  decision_date:
  review_required:
~~~

Apply this gate separately to every source. The default is `unknown` and `incomplete`; missing authority, rights/provenance, sensitivity decision, or review basis stops extraction. Amber proceeds only after its named conditions are satisfied. Red/rejected stops automatic motif/family extraction. In the full workspace, follow `Visual_Resource_Library/CURATION_POLICY.md`; the standalone requirement remains to keep source, function, rights, provenance, restriction, and review evidence. Do not flatten a region or community into labels such as Asian, African, tribal, or mystic.

## 3. Decompose each source into grammar

Do not fuse labels directly. For every source, answer:

~~~text
Context/function          — community, period, use, site/support, audience
Space/composition         — viewpoint, projection, depth/flatness, mass, void
Value structure           — grouping, key, range, accent
Light logic               — source hierarchy, form/shadow relation
Shape/silhouette          — geometry, proportion, distortion, simplification
Line/edge/mark            — ownership, cadence, pressure, hard/soft/lost/found
Color distribution        — hue/value/chroma roles and surround
Material/support/process  — substance, tool, layer, optical behavior
Rhythm/time               — interval, repetition, pause, movement, sequence
Scale/body/gaze           — body-to-world relation and viewer position
Symbol/semantics          — meaning, not merely visible motif
Simplification device     — what the system deliberately omits or compresses
~~~

Name the visual problem each source solves. Run a motif blackout on non-load-bearing motifs. If an authorized motif is itself the text, identity, narrative, ritual, heraldic, or iconographic function, declare it as `essential_authorized_motif`, preserve its context/permission/limits, and test whether the remaining formal/process grammar is still understood. Reject only when a source contributes nothing beyond an unexplained or unauthorized motif.

## 4. Choose one fusion mode

| Mode | Operating structure | Starting ratio hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| `structural_graft` | A owns composition/space; B owns surface/process | about 80/20 to 70/30 decision dominance |
| `surface_inoculation` | A stays intact; B is a bounded accent | about 90/10 or 85/15 |
| `split_domain_coexistence` | each ontology keeps its own grammar; shared physics connects them | no global blend ratio |
| `process_translation` | function/making logic moves into a new medium | mechanism-specific |
| `dialectical_birth` | parent interaction creates new rules | discover rules before locking ratio |

These are calibration hypotheses, not universal laws. `Controlled dialogue` is a ratio/jurisdiction regime inside the five modes, not a sixth mode; a starting point near 65/35 is viable only when the sources own different dimensions.

## 5. Assign jurisdiction

Every load-bearing dimension has a default owner or an explicit co-ownership contract:

~~~yaml
jurisdiction:
  - dimension:
    ownership_mode: default_single | explicit_co_owned
    owner:
    co_owners: []
    owner_authority:
    conflict_resolution:
    final_decision_authority:
    support:
    support_may_change:
    invariant:
    forbidden_bleed:
    visible_proof:
~~~

Audit message/function, hierarchy, space, camera, perspective, value, light, shape, silhouette, line/edge/mark, color, material/process, rhythm, motion, identity/body, ornament/iconography, cultural register, typography, and brand when relevant. Keep them separate when different authorities may own them.

An owner is not limited to parent A/B. Valid authorities may include the current brief, project contract, a dimension-specific reference, a source grammar, the dominant family, or an `emergent_system`. Message belongs to the brief; identity may belong to a reference; brand belongs to the project contract. Lower authorities cannot seize them.

Rules:

- support may alter only the declared sub-property;
- shared is a bridge relation, not an excuse to leave authority or conflict resolution unstated;
- global physics has one authority when all domains must share a world;
- split-domain work needs jurisdiction per domain plus a boundary and authored relationship; physical bridge evidence is required only when domains must share one world;
- source dominance is not measured by prompt word count.

For split-domain work, also declare:

~~~yaml
domains:
  - domain:
    render_grammar:
    jurisdiction:
    forbidden_bleed:
global_physics:
  authority:
  perspective_and_scale:
  gravity_and_contact:
  light_interaction:
  atmosphere_and_motion:
~~~

## 6. State ratio semantics

Never write only `80/20`. Distinguish:

- decision ratio — how much rule authority each source holds;
- salience ratio — perceptual prominence;
- area ratio — frame coverage;
- per-axis ratio — dominance within space, color, edge, material, and other dimensions.

A 5% accent may carry 40% of salience. Test ratios in output rather than inferring them from text length.

## 7. Lock invariant and variation

~~~yaml
fusion_control:
  absolute_invariants:
  relational_invariants:
  bounded_modulation:
  deliberate_open:
  exclusions:
  coupled_axes:
~~~

Relational invariants are preferred for series: for example, the photographic person remains more optically resolved than the painted world even when light, crop, and density change.

Deliberate open is explicitly assigned freedom, not missing instruction. Coupled axes may change together when they test one causal hypothesis: light source + shadow geometry + material highlight; camera distance + perspective + anatomy.

## 8. Design productive tension

Fusion is not averaging. State:

~~~yaml
productive_tensions:
  - id:
    rank_or_counterpoint_voice:
    pole_A:
    pole_B:
    purpose_for_message:
    agreement_bed:
    disagreement_location:
    first_read:
    delayed_read:
    bridge_or_resolution:
    collapse_if_too_low:
    collapse_if_too_high:
~~~

Useful candidate poles include flat/deep, precise/gestural, polished/raw, sacred/domestic, historical/contemporary, still/kinetic, organic/geometric, monumental/intimate, and permanent/ephemeral.

Default to one primary tension. Multiple tensions are allowed when their hierarchy or counterpoint is explicit and each earns a different conceptual function; remove redundant tensions. The agreement bed must keep perspective, scale, contact, light, atmosphere, material evidence, or formal convention coherent enough for the intended world.

## 9. Build a visible bridge

Bridge type:

- shared — both systems use one invariant;
- resonant — a minor behavior in one rhymes with a major behavior in the other;
- engineered — a carrier or cause creates interaction.

Bridge class:

- structural;
- optical;
- spatial;
- material;
- kinetic;
- semantic;
- causal.

~~~yaml
bridge:
  type:
  class:
  tension_resolved:
  shared_invariant:
  carrier:
  crossing_zone:
  visible_proofs:
  predicted_ablation_effect:
  false_bridge_to_avoid:
~~~

A semantic similarity alone is usually weak. Give it structure, material, action, or causal evidence. A third object is a catalyst, not an undeclared third family.

## 10. Translate function, not costume

Use this ladder:

~~~text
observe evidence
→ identify original function
→ extract mechanism and constraint
→ rebuild through the new medium's affordances
→ test equivalent effect
→ preserve context and name the result honestly
~~~

Use two anti-imitation diagnostics:

1. Remove the source name: can the grammar still execute?
2. Remove non-load-bearing recognizable motifs: does the mechanism still live? If an authorized motif is essential, can its function, context, permission, limits, and honest naming be demonstrated?

Reject when the source name is doing all the work, when the only contribution is an unexplained/unauthorized motif, or when the direction uses unauthorized signature compositions, living-artist style substitution, or false authenticity claims.

## 11. Verify emergent rules

Name two or three rules true only of the synthesis.

Example: photographic person in a hand-painted dimensional world.

1. light, shadow, perspective, contact, atmosphere, and wind are shared;
2. material rendering remains intentionally different by domain;
3. environmental bounce affects the person without converting skin, hair, or clothing into paint.

If emergent rules only restate motifs, palette, or labels, the result is decoration or pastiche.

## 12. Family questions

1. What message/function and viewing sequence does the family serve?
2. What is the anchor?
3. What formal/spatial system creates the legible base?
4. What is the precise departure, and how is dominance measured?
5. What invariant relations and bounded variables hold it together?
6. What light, edge, material, and process behavior belongs to this world?
7. What is the productive tension, boundary topology, and bridge?
8. What is the signature move?
9. What is the signature collapse, trigger, and guard?
10. What context, rights, cultural, generator, or model boundaries limit the claim?

A family is not ready until all ten have operational answers.

## 13. Material foraging

Source structural ideas from architecture, engineering, textile/weaving, scientific diagrams, natural growth, geology, musical notation, ritual-object systems, printing/bookmaking, optics, material science, and regional craft traditions.

Extract mechanism, process, and function. Preserve provenance and restrictions. Do not use a living artist's name as a substitute for understanding.

## 14. Calibration and falsification

Before generation, preregister each material test:

~~~yaml
test:
  test_id:
  hypothesis:
  predicted_result:
  falsifier:
  fixed_variables:
  changed_causal_bundle:
  planned_samples:
  acceptance_rule:
  all_result_ids:
  passed:
  failed:
  confounders:
  conclusion:
~~~

Do not report only winning outputs. Preserve the denominator, all result IDs, failed attempts, settings, and confounders. For a serious fusion/family candidate, plan the relevant subset of:

1. parent A baseline;
2. parent B baseline;
3. naive label blend control;
4. jurisdictional fusion;
5. repeated sample;
6. bridge ablation;
7. ratio sweep with named semantics;
8. jurisdiction swap on one dimension;
9. motif blackout;
10. portrait/close, environment/wide, and hard-mechanism tests;
11. second subject or environment transfer;
12. aspect-ratio and difficult-condition stress;
13. one documented failure-to-repair.

One attractive image proves possibility, not a stable family. Repetition only increases evidence when sample count, controls, settings, pass/fail rule, and denominator are recorded. Change one causal hypothesis per retry and record all coupled axes.

## 15. Promotion ladder

| Level | Evidence |
|---|---|
| task note | one result; no stability claim |
| direction candidate | preregistered repeated samples in one structure with denominator and pass/fail evidence |
| project recipe | at least three structures with recorded attempts and no unresolved critical collapse |
| family candidate | three structures with repeated samples, all-result registry, transfer, stress, ablation, and failure-repair evidence |
| registered family | complete charter with stable signature, collapse, trigger, and guard |
| universal candidate | replicated across multiple families/media with explicit exceptions |

If testing is limited to one generator/version, label the scope and date. Do not claim cross-model stability.

## 17. Documentation rule

## U05 interface

Family growth is promoted through evidence records and a scoped handoff. A family charter is not a capability proof; generator feasibility and live status remain in the generator registry and source register.

- one canonical file per family;
- definitions live only in that family file;
- laws are referenced, not copied;
- examples remain non-authoritative;
- version every family and record superseded modules;
- preserve historical packs as archive;
- keep research/context evidence in its proper library;
- register only after the promotion gate passes.
