# 08 ALBUM ENGINE

An album must feel like one project, not one image copied repeatedly and not unrelated images sharing a palette.

## 1. Stable mother versus variable shots

Lock in 母版锁:

- family;
- subject or identity;
- world view;
- material system;
- palette;
- photographic/render language;
- light family;
- retouch texture;
- logo;
- continuity rules;
- shared negative blockers.

Open in 分镜:

- body state;
- gaze and energy;
- gesture;
- one prop class;
- one mechanism;
- shot scale and focal length;
- camera height and angle;
- light motivation;
- scene;
- foreground hook;
- emotional beat.

## 2. Name the attractor

The attractor is the pose/state cluster the model repeatedly chooses.

Examples:

- chest-up three-quarter face, same halo behind head, neutral gaze;
- eyes closed, head back, glass in hand, reclining;
- centered full-body standing, hands near face;
- walking away in every landscape.

Write it down before designing shots.

Attractor quota:

- each attractor element appears at most once or twice;
- never in adjacent shots;
- the cover may intentionally use the strongest attractor once;
- one signature gesture (for example a prop handed toward the lens) appears at most once in the whole album; later shots must change the viewing relationship instead.

## 3. Axis grid

Rotate a majority of axes between adjacent shots.

| Axis | Example values |
|---|---|
| Body | stand, walk, sit, crouch, recline, turn, kneel, lean |
| Gaze/energy | direct, profile, down, distant, alert, calm, amused, severe |
| Prop | none, mirror, book, cup, umbrella, sword, flower, instrument |
| Mechanism | none, aperture, reflection, caustic, shadow, motion trail, forced perspective |
| Camera | macro, close, medium, full, overhead, low, landscape, rear |
| Lens | 24-35, 40-50, 70-85, 100+ millimeter |
| Light | soft side, hard flash, rim, caustic point, window, overcast, sunset, moon |
| Scene | rotate one primary environment |
| Mood | arrival, curiosity, rest, solitude, recovery, closure |

Changing only the background is not variation.

## 4. Narrative arc

Choose one arc.

### Fashion transformation

icon → detail → expansion → fracture → illusion → motion → negative space → closure

### Calm photobook

morning arrival → slow walk → small discovery → rest → travel → rain → sunset → warm interior → night closure

### Power portrait

reveal → authority → confrontation → vulnerability → recovery → sovereign ending

### Material study

source → close behavior → transition → full silhouette → optical proof → abstract reduction

## 5. Shot-role bank

A balanced 10-12 shot album may include:

1. cover/icon;
2. extreme close beauty;
3. strict profile;
4. material detail;
5. full-body structure;
6. environmental wide;
7. overhead;
8. mirror or optical mechanism;
9. motion;
10. negative-space spread;
11. quiet recovery;
12. closing rear or unresolved symbol.

Do not use all merely because they exist. Select roles that support the arc.

## 6. Calibration wave

Before a full album, test three or four shots that stress different failure modes:

- face/identity close-up;
- full-body anatomy and material;
- difficult mechanism;
- dynamic or environmental shot.

If the mother survives these, build the rest.

## 7. Per-shot rules

Each 分镜 should state:

- forced body/camera state;
- one dominant mechanism;
- one scene;
- one light motivation;
- depth plan;
- one anti-attractor line if risk is high.

Two per-shot disciplines:

- Lock the imaging mechanism, redesign the on-site light. The mother may lock the medium response (CCD/direct-flash contrast, grain, retouch texture) verbatim, but every shot re-designs its own light direction, strength, placement, and highlight width for its scale and action. Copying one flash setup across all shot scales flattens the series and breaks close-ups (see the skin-reflection guard in library/11).
- A prop pool is a pool, not a checklist: one main prop plus at most two or three supporting elements per shot; never stuff the entire pool into one frame.

Useful escalation:

~~~text
强制[本镜头状态]，不得回到[默认吸引子]。
~~~

## 8. Continuity

Decide whether the album preserves:

- exact same person;
- same project but varied models;
- same wardrobe;
- wardrobe variants within one material DNA;
- same day/time arc;
- recurring symbol;
- recurring object placement;
- exact hair continuity.

Do not accidentally randomize identity if the user expects one person.

## 9. Photobook distinction

Photobook language favors:

- behavior over fashion pose;
- off-camera gaze;
- imperfect but coherent framing;
- natural action;
- quiet environmental sound;
- low event, high sensory presence;
- moderate detail and restrained grade.

Fashion album language may be more sculptural, but should still rotate body, camera, and energy.

## 10. Batch validation

## U05 interface

An album uses one series control contract plus per-shot iteration records. Continuity checks record shot IDs, preserved invariants, controlled variations, and the denominator rather than relying on a single representative frame.

Fail conditions:

- too cloned;
- too scattered;
- attractor repetition;
- scene changes without body/camera changes;
- identity drift;
- material signature disappears;
- every shot has equal intensity;
- no opening or ending function.

A successful album has family resemblance, internal contrast, and an emotional sequence.
