# FAMILY — ZEN ARCHITECTURAL POETICS / 禅意建筑诗学

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

Minimal modern architecture, landscape, geometry, and negative space dominate; a real human acts as emotional and scale anchor.

## 1. Core DNA

~~~yaml
read: space-first or negative-space-first
anchor: human scale and one emotional gesture
materials: concrete, stone, wood, glass, still water
geometry: wall, arch, corridor, stair, roofline, platform
palette: mist white, cool jade, warm gray, restrained wood
light: natural morning, overcast, or reflected courtyard light
~~~

## 2. Composition

- human usually 8-25%;
- one or two dominant geometric masses;
- large quiet void;
- asymmetric placement;
- real architectural scale;
- foreground/middle/distance;
- no decorative clutter.

## 3. Human

Adult, calm, off-camera gaze, slow behavior. Wardrobe may be restrained modern or xianxia-modern but must not dominate the architecture.

## 4. Light

Architecture receives motivated daylight with controlled studio-level separation. Water and glass provide limited reflection. Mist is thin.

## 5. Shot bank

- long glass corridor;
- wet courtyard reflection;
- stone platform by still water;
- museum atrium with one paper plane;
- white wall and single tree shadow;
- stair void;
- roofline and distant figure.

## 6. Signature collapse

- generic luxury building photo;
- dead empty minimalism;
- human too large;
- random furniture;
- one-note beige/gray;
- zen decoration without spatial logic;
- heavy fog;
- flat geometry.
