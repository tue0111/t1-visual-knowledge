# FAMILY — NARRATIVE COMIC / 叙事漫画页面

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

A page or panel sequence translates a supplied story beat into readable visual narrative while preserving character, page, scene, and continuity DNA.

## 1. Authority

When a story/MD source is supplied:

- source page content wins;
- do not continue errors from previous images;
- preserve named characters and scene state;
- current correction wins.

## 2. Read order

The story beat is first. Beauty and style support action readability.

Each panel must answer:

- who acts;
- what changes;
- where attention moves;
- what emotional beat closes the panel.

## 3. Style branches

### Modern color comic

Clean line, strong lighting blocks, readable panels, restrained detail.

### Hyper-real manga

Manga anatomy and panel drama with realistic material volume and color.

### Manhwa/webtoon

Vertical readability, clean beauty lines, soft gradients, emotional close-ups.

## 4. Page construction

Lock:

- page number;
- panel count;
- panel order;
- character design;
- dialogue placement policy;
- color/black-and-white mode;
- continuity.

Do not generate text inside art unless explicitly requested and the model can handle it. Keep dialogue as post-production reference by default.

## 5. Special content

For microscopic/body-interior fantasy, use non-graphic landscape logic. Organic forms become terrain, not gore.

## 6. Signature collapse

- wrong page action;
- unreadable panels;
- character drift;
- accidental extra characters;
- text corruption;
- previous output overrides source;
- all panels same shot scale;
- beauty portrait replacing story.
