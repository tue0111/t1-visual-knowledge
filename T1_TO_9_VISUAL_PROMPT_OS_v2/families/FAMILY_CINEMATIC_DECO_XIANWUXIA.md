# FAMILY — CINEMATIC DECO XIANWUXIA / 电影感装饰艺术仙武侠

Version 1.0.0 — promoted to canonical 2026-08-25 through `core/07_FUSION_AND_GROWTH_ENGINE.md` on workspace evidence: three generated albums (剑影浮世， 仙土幻境， 夜人行)， a debug lineage (DarkDeco album v1 → root variant → v2), and a full charter written under core/07 §8 before generation.

## 0. Definition

A real photographic martial-immortal subject (剑客， 女侠， 修士) inhabits a cinematic **two-dimensional Dark-Deco** world spanning jianghu (客栈， 竹林， 屋瓦， 崖壁， 渡口) and heaven (云海， 浮山， 天门). Martial and spiritual force renders as **flat Art-Deco geometric energy**, never photoreal smoke or particle. One physical world, two render regimes: person real, world flat.

This is not xianxia editorial in real space (`families/FAMILY_XIANXIA_EDITORIAL.md`) and not photoreal costume drama. The identity of this family = **force drawn as Deco geometry + explicit cinematic camera/lens/VFX grammar**.

## 1. Core DNA

- anchor: real photographic subject — face, hands, body contact, real silk/leather garment behavior; the only volumetric presence in frame;
- world: 2D dark-deco silhouette planes, thick smooth lines, 2–3 large flat color masses per composition;
- base/break ratio hypothesis: ~75% Deco two-dimensional rendering language / ~25% xianwuxia imagery plus real anchor; calibrate per brief, never present as universal constant (core/01);
- named energy system, one per realm: 剑气＝旭日放射； 轻功气流＝流线； 内力＝同心脉冲； 云海＝chevron层叠；
- palette base: 墨黑， 靛蓝， 月白， 雾青， 旧金； one dominant accent color per realm (青玉， 朱砂， 金， 紫， 碧， 月银， 血红)； accents stay local, never flood skin;
- structural rhyme (fusion bridge axis): martial motion and internal force translate into flat geometric paths; this is the load-bearing translation of the whole family;
- absurd permission: impossible geometry (倒悬， 无尽天梯， 递归， 矛盾透视) is an authored decision and must survive; semantic correction of the impossible is a failure mode.

## 2. Render boundary and physical bridge

Boundary: person = photographic cinema; world and energy = flat Deco graphic. The two grammars must not bleed into each other (no outline on skin, no photoreal texture on architecture).

Physical bridge (shared-world integration is intended): shared perspective, one motivated key-light direction, consistent ground scale, contact shadow, occlusion, aerial depth haze, common wind and momentum direction.

## 3. Camera, lens, and VFX grammar

One authored camera intent per shot; effects need optical evidence at highlight edges, never a uniform filter:

- camera positions: hero low angle, dutch angle, overhead tilt-shift miniature, macro close-up, extreme wide dynamic;
- lens behaviors: anamorphic bokeh, tilt-shift plane, controlled wide-angle (guard face distortion);
- perceptual VFX: bloom/halation, chromatic aberration, motion blur, veiling glare — applied only where the optical cause exists;
- depth is authored by layering, focus planes, and haze, because the world itself is flat.

## 4. Light

Cinematic motivated sources (moon, lanterns, dawn, thunder, blade glint); dramatic chiaroscuro with rim separation; the flat world answers with same-direction plane highlights; background retreats unless the brief demands a flat high-key reading.
