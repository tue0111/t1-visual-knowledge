# FAMILY — PHONE-FLASH JEWELRY BOUDOIR / 手机闪光珠宝闺阁

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

A believable high-end smartphone rear-camera flash snapshot inside a dim antique jewelry room. The subject is softly lifted by direct white flash; the room remains subdued, readable, and atmospheric.

## 1. Core DNA

~~~yaml
anchor: face and quiet snapshot presence
camera: small-sensor 24-28 millimeter equivalent
light: soft direct front phone flash
depth: relatively deep; no strong portrait bokeh
contrast: low
saturation: low to medium-low
sharpness: detailed but not professional razor-sharp
background: dim, muted, readable
~~~

## 2. Is / is not

Is:

- phone-photo perspective;
- slight softness;
- mild flash dynamic range;
- real glass, jade, pearl, lacquer reflections;
- natural snapshot gesture;
- quiet luxury.

Is not:

- DSLR portrait;
- 85-millimeter compression;
- studio softbox;
- cinematic rim light;
- room-wide bright flash;
- wedding photography;
- cheap boudoir.

## 3. Scene vocabulary

Choose a limited subset:

- black lacquer cabinet;
- glass jewelry display;
- pearl necklace;
- jade ornament;
- porcelain vase;
- antique perfume bottle;
- jewelry box;
- small warm practical lamp;
- dark stone floor.

Do not activate all textures equally.

## 4. Subject and wardrobe

Adult subject, soft mature beauty, real skin. Refined ancient-inspired or quiet luxury dress. Sensuality remains non-explicit and material-led.

## 5. Light

~~~text
柔和白色手机直闪主要集中于人物，使脸部、手势与服装从较暗房间中浮现；背景降低亮度、对比与饱和度，但珠宝柜、玻璃、玉器与暖色实景灯仍可辨认。禁止全室闪光溢出。
~~~

## 6. Pose

Natural phone-caught action:

- resting hand on cabinet edge;
- adjusting scarf or sleeve;
- looking slightly away;
- turning after being called;
- sitting near display;
- walking past glass.

## 7. Signature collapse

- too DSLR;
- too cinematic;
- background too bright;
- background disappears;
- flash too hard or too broad;
- image too crisp;
- image too dreamy;
- gold becomes overexposed;
- wardrobe becomes bridal.
