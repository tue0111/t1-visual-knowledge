# FAMILY — OUTDOOR SNAPSHOT PHOTOBOOK / 户外抓拍写真

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

A real outdoor fashion or travel moment retains hard sun, imperfect crop, grain, compression, and environmental pressure instead of becoming a clean catalogue image.

## 1. Core DNA

- real outdoor capture;
- candid or accidental fashion behavior;
- 28-50 millimeter natural perspective;
- hard sun, overcast, or weather-specific motivated light;
- fine grain and slight digital/scan compression;
- imperfect but intentional crop;
- environment as narrative space;
- no studio polish.

## 2. Color branches

### Dense cool landscape

Deep cobalt/navy sky, cold water, muted olive/brown land, natural warm skin.

### Calm photobook

Soft green, sky blue, ivory, muted yellow/orange accents, moderate contrast.

### Rain

Cool gray, moss green, warm wood, controlled wet reflection.

## 3. Body language

- walking;
- pausing;
- sitting;
- looking away;
- reading;
- adjusting garment;
- resting;
- crossing a path.

Avoid repeated camera-facing model poses.

## 4. Camera

Allow:

- cropped mountain or body edge;
- hair shadow across face;
- uneven sun;
- foreground grass;
- imperfect horizon when motivated;
- moderate depth rather than constant bokeh.

## 5. Signature collapse

- clean catalogue;
- postcard landscape;
- over-polished skin;
- soft studio bloom;
- travel advertisement;
- every shot walking away;
- same scenery and pose;
- fake film preset.
