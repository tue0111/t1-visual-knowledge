# FAMILY — MONUMENTAL SCULPTURAL RELIEF / 纪念性浮雕转译

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

An existing two-dimensional comic, ink, or illustration composition is preserved while selected forms gain two-point-five-dimensional sculptural thickness, material weight, shadow, and monumental scale.

## 1. Core rule

Upgrade material and depth. Do not redesign composition.

Preserve:

- camera angle;
- subject placement;
- silhouette;
- radiating arm/wing/structure pattern;
- foreground scale;
- sky and mountain placement;
- line and hatching logic;
- action direction.

Add:

- thickness;
- carved volume;
- material behavior;
- cast shadow;
- atmospheric scale;
- restrained edge light.

## 2. Layer contract

- original line/ink/composition remains the flat design authority;
- major figure becomes sculptural relief;
- foreground and environment gain enough depth to prove scale;
- do not convert the whole frame into photoreal CGI.

## 3. Material branches

- weathered sacred wood;
- stone and mineral;
- bronze relief;
- lacquered carved panel;
- paper/ink relief;
- fossilized organic structure.

Choose one dominant material family.

## 4. Camera and scale

Low angle and environmental scale anchors are common. The monumental subject should overwhelm terrain without losing the original composition.

## 5. Signature collapse

- new composition invented;
- full CGI replacement;
- original line language erased;
- relief remains flat;
- random texture overload;
- subject loses monumental scale;
- copied IP details drift.

## 6. Guard

~~~text
只升级体积、厚度、材质、光影与空间深度；严格保留参考图构图、镜头、主体位置、外轮廓、动作方向与平面线稿逻辑。主要形体获得二点五维雕塑厚度和真实投影，但不得重画为全新场景或纯三维游戏画面。
~~~

## 7. Family negative add-on

~~~text
重新设计构图，改变镜头角度，主体位置漂移，轮廓错误，动作方向改变，平面线稿完全消失，纯三维游戏渲染，塑料模型，浮雕没有厚度，投影缺失，纹理噪声过多，尺度不宏大。
~~~
