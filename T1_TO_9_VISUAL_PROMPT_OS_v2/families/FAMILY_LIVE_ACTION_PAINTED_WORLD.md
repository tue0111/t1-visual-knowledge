# FAMILY — LIVE-ACTION PAINTED WORLD / 真人入绘境

Version 1.0.0

## 0. Definition

A real photographic human lives inside a navigable three-dimensional world whose surfaces remain hand-painted animation. Rendering differs; physics is shared.

## 1. Signature

~~~text
同光、同影、同透视、同尺度、同空气、同风；人物写真，世界手绘。
~~~

This family is not a 2D backdrop composite. It is one physical world with two render regimes.

## 2. Core DNA

~~~yaml
anchor: real human identity and body contact
dominant_read: scene-first or balanced environmental portrait
base: 70-80% hand-painted three-dimensional world
break: 20-30% live-action photographic human
render_boundary:
  human: photographic skin, hair, garment
  world: painted textures, simplified shading, stylized forms
physical_bridge:
  - perspective
  - sunlight
  - ground scale
  - contact shadow
  - occlusion
  - bounce light
  - atmospheric depth
  - common wind
signature_system: shared-wind resonance
~~~

## 3. World behavior

The environment has:

- walkable three-dimensional terrain;
- watercolor/gouache-like hand-painted texture;
- simplified shape language;
- soft cel-like value grouping;
- layered foreground, middle, and distance;
- luminous but restrained natural palette;
- stylized plants and creatures with real growth, gravity, and occlusion.

It is not a flat illustration, photoreal landscape, or plastic game render.

## 4. Human behavior

The human remains:

- real photographic anatomy;
- real skin and hair;
- real garment fiber and folds;
- natural unposed behavior;
- physically grounded.

The human is not outlined, toon-shaded, anime-faced, or rendered as a game character.

## 5. Integration proof

Show at least five:

- shoe compresses grass;
- grass overlaps ankle;
- shared shadow direction;
- green or sky bounce enters real garment;
- foreground leaves occlude the body;
- rain or mist crosses both regimes;
- reflection includes the human correctly;
- hair, dress, grass, leaves, curtains, and clouds share wind.

## 6. Background and read order

For photobook:

1. environment and light;
2. real human discovery;
3. path and small life;
4. distant world.

Human scale usually 25-45% of frame height. Close shots are allowed as rhythm breaks.

## 7. Camera language

Natural environmental photography:

- 35-50 millimeter for walking and space;
- 70-85 millimeter for quiet medium portraits;
- moderate depth of field;
- no constant fashion posing;
- foreground plant frame;
- calm negative space.

## 8. Album arcs

### Calm photobook

meadow morning → forest → flowers → stream → lake rest → train → rain village → sea rest → field → sunset → warm interior → firefly night

### Discovery

arrival → scale surprise → creature encounter → weather change → shelter → farewell

## 9. Signature collapse

| Collapse | Trigger | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| human becomes anime | no human render boundary | forbid stylization on skin/hair/clothes |
| world becomes photo | world behavior too generic | lock hand-painted texture and simplified shading |
| green-screen look | no bridge | add contact, shadow, occlusion, bounce, atmosphere |
| flat background | no walkable depth | terrain, foreground/middle/distance |
| game screenshot | interface/character copying | ignore text/UI and original characters |
| generic fantasy | style label only | describe spatial, surface, and light mechanisms |
| photobook becomes fashion ad | posed gaze/body | behavior, off-camera gaze, low-event moments |
