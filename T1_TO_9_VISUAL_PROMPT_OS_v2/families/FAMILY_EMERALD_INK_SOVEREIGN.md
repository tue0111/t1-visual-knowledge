# FAMILY — EMERALD INK SOVEREIGN / 醉墨翡君

Version 2.0.0  
Derived from the verified legacy family.

## 0. Definition

A photographic cinematic sovereign whose face and upper body remain real while motion blur, hair, garment, and environment dissolve outward into directional emerald brushwork.

## 1. Core DNA

~~~yaml
anchor: thin-flash-lit face and upper body
base: 65-70% cinematic photographic capture
break: about 20% directional paint dissolve
optional: 10-15% one illusion mechanism
bridge: optical motion blur and brush stroke share one vector
palette: emerald, black jade, dark teal, oxidized silver, charcoal
skin_rule: neutral, never green or warm orange
camera: 35-50 millimeter
~~~

## 2. Emergent rules

1. distance from the face controls degree of paint dissolve;
2. real motion blur becomes brushwork;
3. hair, fabric, atmosphere, and paint share one direction;
4. depth of field behaves like a brush opening outward;
5. face never dissolves.

## 3. Subject and wardrobe

Adult East Asian woman, mature, cold, sovereign. Minimal xianxia robe in emerald/black-jade/charcoal, weighted silk or washed satin, strong shoulder and long-line silhouette.

## 4. Light

Refined frontal flash on the real anchor; background retreats. One motivated source per shot may support moon, candle, water, or architecture.

## 5. Variation axes

- body: stand, stride, lean, sit, turn, recline, rotate;
- energy: alert, severe, amused, tired, reflective, triumphant;
- mechanism: edge dispersion, motion trail, double exposure, caustic, moire, glitch, mirror;
- scene: stone hall, cellar, curtain chamber, moon window, corridor, table, throne;
- camera: 35-50 millimeter, varied height and crop.

Attractor: eyes closed, head back, glass, passive recline. Limit to one shot.

## 6. Signature collapse

- whole image becomes illustration;
- whole image stays clean photo;
- face dissolves;
- green skin;
- warm orange oil-paint cast;
- repeated attractor;
- motion vector fragments.
