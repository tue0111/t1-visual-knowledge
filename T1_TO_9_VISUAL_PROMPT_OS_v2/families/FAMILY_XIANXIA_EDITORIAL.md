# FAMILY — XIANXIA / ANCIENT CHINESE FASHION EDITORIAL / 古风仙侠时尚摄影

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

A real adult subject inhabits a motivated ancient Chinese cinematic space, wearing premium silk/gauze couture whose material, pose, camera, and light create the fantasy rather than generic effects.

## 1. Core DNA

- adult East Asian subject;
- mature, calm, restrained expression;
- real skin and hair;
- silk, gauze, draped bands, wide sleeves, layered hem;
- real architecture or controlled studio narrative;
- foreground/middle/distance;
- pure ancient environment when requested;
- no generic studio hanfu.

## 2. Read modes

### Face-first editorial

Use 70-100 millimeter, controlled background, beauty light.

### Cinematic scene

Use 24-50 millimeter, foreground lead, motivated action, narrative space.

### Hard-flash snapshot

Use close wide perspective, direct flash, underexposed ambient background, clear support points.

## 3. Wardrobe

Write behavior before label:

- weighted silk drape;
- translucent gauze overlap;
- directional sheen;
- embroidered line with real fiber;
- sleeve and trailing fabric as composition lead.

Avoid costume-shop decoration and wedding-dress drift.

## 4. Pose

Behavior before anatomy jargon. State:

- what the subject was doing;
- where weight is supported;
- what the hand touches;
- gaze target;
- how garment follows movement.

Use biomechanics only to repair failure.

## 5. Scene branches

- waterside bridge or banquet;
- palace chamber;
- bamboo forest;
- courtyard after rain;
- moon window;
- mountain terrace;
- underwater palace, with restrained particles and clear water.

One shot uses one primary scene.

## 6. Light branches

- soft directional side light;
- architectural daylight;
- direct on-camera hard flash;
- phone flash only if the family intentionally shifts;
- moon plus motivated warm practical;
- water caustic for a specific mechanism.

## 7. Signature collapse

- tourist ancient costume;
- generic xianxia portrait;
- wedding dress;
- cheap sensual staging;
- background empty or dead black;
- excessive warm gold;
- smoke wall;
- wide-angle anatomy failure;
- modern object leakage.
