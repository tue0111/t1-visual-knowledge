## 0. Definition

A game, anime, manga, or original character is translated into a real photographic adult model while immutable character DNA survives through silhouette, color blocks, identity prop, and materialized costume architecture.

## 1. Core formula

~~~text
角色 DNA + 真人成年面孔 + 身份道具轴线 + 可读轮廓 + 高定材质化服装 + 分层摄影灯光 + 后退背景
~~~

## 2. Read order

Default:

1. face and gaze;
2. identity prop or mark;
3. silhouette and costume architecture;
4. atmosphere/world cue.

Prop-axis may lead only if it returns the eye to the face.

## 4. Translation rules

Do not merely dress a model in a costume. Translate:

- armor into premium metal/fabric/resin behavior;
- magic into one controlled local light/material cue;
- exaggerated game silhouette into wearable high-fashion architecture;
- identity prop into a composition axis;
- character emotion into gaze and body energy.

## 5. Camera

- 70-85 millimeter for face-first;
- 50 millimeter for face plus costume;
- 24-35 millimeter for dynamic prop/body, with face protected;
- low angle distorts foreground, not facial geometry.

## 6. Light

Beauty key establishes the human. Edge light separates silhouette. Signature glow supports DNA only. Background remains an optical container unless world narrative is required.

## 7. Sensuality

Comes from structure, cut, material, posture, gaze, and authority. Never let the body replace character identity.

## 8. Signature collapse

- generic fantasy woman;
- missing identity prop;
- cheap cosplay;
- game splash-art CGI;
- cute idol drift;
- armor becomes bulky;
- bikini/lingerie logic;
- prop covers face;
- too many magic effects.
