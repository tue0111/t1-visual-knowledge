# FAMILY — PICTORIAL REALITY / 拼贴实境·三界一身

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

A scene-first real architectural world is physically interrupted by printed, painted, cut, sculptural, and absolute flat elements. Reality is cut and quietly reassembled.

## 1. Core read

1. space and abnormal reality;
2. overscale material intervention;
3. real human anchor;
4. edge, thickness, shadow, and flatness evidence.

Human scale often occupies 8-22% of the frame.

## 2. Three ontologies

### Real layer

Person, architecture, ground, light, and camera perspective remain photographic.

### Material collage layer

Paper, canvas, fabric, plaster, printed imagery, or relief:

- visible edge;
- finite thickness;
- slight lift;
- print/fiber texture;
- shared light;
- real cast shadow.

### Absolute flat layer

Circle, strip, rectangle, or line:

- zero thickness;
- zero shadow;
- unaffected by scene light;
- sharp edge;
- appears on the uppermost image plane.

## 3. Required illusion evidence

A strong image uses at least four:

- scale displacement;
- spatial displacement;
- ontology displacement;
- semantic displacement;
- garment crossover;
- delayed rereading.

## 4. Composition

Preferred:

- 16:9 or 4:3;
- asymmetry;
- corridor/arch geometry;
- negative space;
- wet reflection;
- distant human;
- foreground occlusion;
- one dominant impossible intervention.

## 5. Light

Architecture receives controlled natural light. Material cutouts share source and shadow. Flat graphics remain physically unaffected.

## 6. Material branches

### Asian

Rice paper, ink, silk, lacquer, jade, cinnabar, scroll fragments, cloud and garden motifs.

### European

Canvas, tapestry, fresco sky, marble fragments, classical floral still life, stone, old gold.

### Vietnamese/Indochine

Aged lime wall, dark wood shutter, ceramic tile, lotus pond, banana-leaf shadow, lacquer painting, mother-of-pearl glint, humid air.

## 7. Garment crossover

A real hem, sleeve, or shawl transitions at a visible seam into printed paper, tapestry, landscape, or painted cloth. The seam must prove cut edge, thickness, fiber, and shadow.

## 8. GPT semantic-correction defense

If the model rationalizes collage into a mural or real scenery, add:

~~~text
这是刻意的纸片拼贴艺术，不是语义连贯的真实场景，也不是墙上壁画。超尺度景物必须是覆盖在建筑前方的印刷纸片，保留明显剪裁边、纸张厚度、拼贴接缝和统一方向的实体投影；不得融入背景成为连续插画。人物保持中远景小比例。绝对平面色形保持零厚度、零投影。
~~~

## 9. Signature collapse

- mural instead of collage;
- flat sticker without thickness;
- random scrapbook;
- portrait-first drift;
- human enlarged;
- flat shape gains volume;
- absurdity corrected away;
- too many materials.

## 10. Family negative add-on

~~~text
普通人像封面，人物占据画面中心大面积，廉价贴纸拼贴，随机剪贴簿，剪贴变成壁画，纸边消失，纸张厚度消失，投影消失，拼贴接缝消失，所有元素无缝融合成连续插画，绝对平面图形长出厚度或投影，透视不统一，光线不统一，纹理墙。
~~~

## 11. Fallback compositing

When one-pass generation cannot preserve all ontologies:

1. generate real person plus architecture;
2. generate material cutouts separately;
3. create flat graphics directly;
4. composite with controlled edges and shadows.

This is not failure. It is assigning each medium to the tool best able to preserve it.
