# FAMILY — MIXED-MEDIA CONSTRUCTED REALITY / 混合媒介构成实境

Version 2.0.0

## 0. Definition

A real photographic anchor occupies a controlled diorama of flat paper, two-point-five-dimensional resin/glass/film, and sparse foreground optical elements.

## 1. Core DNA

- face-first by default;
- real photographic face;
- flat dark or symbolic background;
- one two-point-five-dimensional material bridge;
- two or three active texture families;
- controlled bubbles/particles;
- no ordinary room unless requested.

## 2. Layer model

- 2D: paper field, pattern, symbol, silhouette.
- 2.5D: resin, acetate, glass petal, mylar-like film, relief.
- 3D: real face/body and physical garment.
- foreground: sparse blurred material edge or bubble.
- atmosphere: low-detail optical binder.

## 3. Material selection

Choose one dominant bridge:

- jade glass petals;
- transparent resin ribbons;
- opalescent film;
- lacquer silhouette;
- cut paper;
- controlled bubbles;
- crystal geometry.

Do not give every material equal weight.

## 4. Light

The real face receives the primary key. Transparent materials receive edge/refractive light. Flat background remains low-detail. Bubbles and particles only reveal depth.

## 5. Signature collapse

- face turns illustrated;
- background becomes ordinary room;
- all materials become plastic;
- bubble wall;
- random scrapbook;
- style pile;
- no shared perspective/light;
- flat 2.5D;
- text layout drift.
