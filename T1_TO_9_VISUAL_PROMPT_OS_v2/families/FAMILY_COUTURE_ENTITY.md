# FAMILY — COUTURE ENTITY / 高定实体流

Version 1.0.0  
Supersedes the narrower creature-only family inside this pack.

## 0. Definition

A real photographic fashion subject is wrapped by one semi-real, two-point-five-dimensional entity whose structure transforms into one named couture material system, supported by one restrained flat graphic symbol.

The entity may be an animal, plant, artifact, natural phenomenon, or metaphysical carrier.

## 1. Is / is not

Is:

- real human anchor;
- one readable second subject;
- one continuous material transition;
- real anatomy or construction logic;
- dark or controlled visual field;
- premium mixed-media fashion;
- face-first by default.

Is not:

- beauty plus pet plus smoke plus flowers;
- random fantasy props;
- generic ancient costume;
- monster design;
- flower wall;
- pure illustration;
- armor covering the whole body;
- vague magical energy.

## 2. Core DNA

~~~yaml
anchor: real face, eyes, expression
read_order:
  first: face
  second: entity core and structural curve
  third: garment architecture
  fourth: named material flow
  fifth: flat symbol/background
base_break:
  base: 60-70% photographic subject and garment
  break: 20-30% entity/material sculpture
  graphic: about 10%
signature: entity form becomes material flow and returns the eye to the face
eye_lock: mandatory — see library/11 Eye-life lock.
~~~

## 3. Layer contract

- 3D: human, skin, hair, physical garment.
- 2.5D: entity sculpture, finite thickness, shared light, cast shadow.
- 2D: one circle, band, line, or field; zero thickness and shadow.

All transition behavior follows core/05_LAYER_REALITY_ENGINE.md.

## 4. Entity logic

### Creature

Anatomy creates the curve.

### Plant

Growth creates the curve.

### Artifact

Construction, folding, fracture, orbit, or reflection creates the curve.

### Phenomenon

Causal physics creates the field.

### Metaphysical concept

A visible carrier makes the concept renderable.

## 5. Named-system formula

~~~text
[实体核心]、[软性材料]、[环境成员]与[笔触或图形成员]融合成一股连续的“[系统名称]”。它们共享同一流向、透视、主光与转化边界，不是并列道具。
~~~

Examples:

- 锦鲤绸鳍流;
- 蛇烟丝绸流;
- 鹤羽道气流;
- 莲汐水绸流;
- 瓷金绸流;
- 蚀冠玄纱流;
- 琴弦共振膜;
- 影生黑绒流.

## 6. Wardrobe

Use cultural silhouette only after optical behavior:

~~~text
[文化或建筑轮廓] + 高定结构 + 可读的奢华材质行为
~~~

The wardrobe must echo the entity without becoming a costume-shop imitation.

White-garment guard（白色服装强制）：白色必须洁白发光、真实 anisotropic sheen；背景压暗2–2.5档衬托；白衣绝不发灰、发脏、塑料化。

## 7. Light

Default studio relationship:

~~~text
大型柔光箱照亮真人脸部、肩颈与关键材质；负补光雕刻骨相与服装结构；极窄轮廓光描出实体边缘、薄膜与金属线。背景比人物暗二至二点五档。
~~~

## 9. Signature collapses

| Collapse | Trigger | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| prop pile | members listed separately | one named system |
| entity becomes pet | no couture/sculptural role | second-read sculpture, transition, shared light |
| face becomes illustration | material style bleeds inward | real-face hard boundary |
| pattern becomes sticker | no internal optical continuity | motif grows inside material |
| artifact becomes armor | rigid layer covers body | asymmetric partial form, rigid-to-soft transition |
| plant becomes flower wall | no core growth path | one plant, one vector |
| phenomenon becomes glow | no causal source | source and field physics |
| symbol becomes clutter | many icons | one symbol, one transformation |
