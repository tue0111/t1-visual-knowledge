# Candid photography — scoped practitioner presets

Status: COMMUNITY_HEURISTIC, adapted 2026-09-11; not a new universal law or measured model capability. Load for photographic prompt repair, summer candid, or staged observer-style cosplay. Do not apply to an unrelated brief merely because a character is mentioned.

## Visual outcome before camera labels — Nanyuan

First decide whether the image serves the person or the environment. Attach action and attributes to their owner. Specify subject size in frame, viewpoint and desired spatial relationships separately: a wide shot is not the same decision as a wide-angle lens. Describe visible sharpness, motion and lighting before adding camera numbers; those numbers are cues, not a physical simulation guarantee. A lens/look name should have a visible consequence that remains useful if the name is poorly understood. When a detail disappears, repair its priority or relationship before adding more words.

Use this as a diagnostic approach, not a compulsory six-part format. The author's explanation of model training is an inference. Midjourney flags/version examples require current model/tool documentation before use; they never become generic image-tool parameters.

## Summer companion candid — 94vanAI

Use clearly adult subjects and a summer-swimwear brief. One requested photograph can contain several people in one shared event; it does not require a grid. Give each person separate identity, character cues and garment design. Assign each reference a role so layout or pose does not silently replace identity. Coordinate reactions around one event. Let wet hair, fabric and reflections follow that event; choose a principal imaging medium and restrained photographic imperfections. A companion viewpoint conveys familiarity through distance, gesture and gaze rather than a catalogue pose.

These are preset choices. The source's age range, no-text rule, focal-length range and single-image preference are not universal OS requirements. Do not infer missing character details as verified facts or use an automatic age-up instruction to sexualize a minor character. Use clearly adult characters/models for this preset.

## Staged observer candid — VoxcatAI

Use fictional, staged adult cosplay in a public-looking setting. Build the impression of incidental observation through an obstruction, reflection, distance, off-centre crop or an interrupted action. Keep identity readable despite the obstruction. Character personality should inform action and gaze. An occasional glance toward the camera can supply a distinct narrative beat. For a requested series, vary viewpoint, distance, action, orientation, light and framing meaningfully; changing only the background is insufficient.

The author's obstruction percentages and discovered-camera frequency are optional composition heuristics, not hard gates or measured success rates. Keep this a photographic fiction, not instructions for actual covert recording or private-space voyeurism. Default wardrobe is fully worn; observer style does not imply sexual exposure.

## Integration decisions for this OS

The following are local design decisions, not quotations or claimed author prescriptions:

- Select one dominant family. For a travel snapshot use the snapshot grammar; for cosplay-led work use the appropriate character family. A preset may supply a scoped exception, not silently replace the family.
- When the current brief explicitly requests character-inspired swimwear, preserve reference identity and selected character cues while translating garment construction. Do not inherit the editorial family's anti-bikini-armour negative as a ban on requested swimwear. Retain protections against identity loss, anatomy failure and irrelevant body-part framing. Outside this explicit preset, the existing family remains unchanged.
- Record subject count, output count and layout separately. Three adults in one image remain one continuous scene. An album request creates separate frames; a user-requested contact sheet is a distinct layout choice, not silently forbidden by this preset.
- A reference-role assignment limits transfer. Do not infer ethnicity, consent, unseen details or exact identity from a source image. Maintain uncertainty where evidence is absent.
- Preserve user language/branding/layout overrides. Otherwise the project's zh-CN three-section prompt and literal `T1 to 9` default remain; the source's no-text preference does not automatically remove the brand. “Film” or “CCD” describes appearance, not an instruction to add UI, borders or a contact sheet.
- For a scene-led wide composition, detail distant people only to the resolution needed by the brief. Specify subject scale and spatial relationships; do not force face-first framing through excess facial detail.
- Keep candid imperfections causal and local. Blur, grain or imperfect framing never excuse fused bodies, swapped identities or unreadable required details. Perspective depends on camera placement; focal-length words alone do not prove geometry.
- Route prompt-authoring, generation, editing and analysis through the existing task/tool contract. These presets do not authorize generation when the user requested only prompts, or prompt-only replies when a supported generation task was requested.
