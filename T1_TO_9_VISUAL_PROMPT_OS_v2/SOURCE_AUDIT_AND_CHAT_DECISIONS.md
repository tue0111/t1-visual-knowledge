## 4. Decisions recovered from chat

These are now hard project rules:

1. Generated image prompts must be Simplified Chinese.
2. Ordinary English photographic terms are translated when possible.
3. Prompt delivery uses three sections only:
   - 母版锁
   - 分镜
   - 通用负面提示词
4. The T1 to 9 logo belongs inside 母版锁, not a fourth section.
5. The negative prompt explicitly permits only T1 to 9.
6. Album shots are separated for easy copying.
7. Do not replace chat output with a text file when the user asks to see the prompt.
8. Reference typography is ignored unless requested.
9. Creature couture expands to plants, artifacts, natural phenomena, and metaphysical carriers.
10. Blue-White Gold-Seam uses one gold line crossing three-dimensional embroidery, two-point-five-dimensional porcelain repair, and a flat two-dimensional line.
11. Cross-medium images require:
    - a render boundary;
    - a physical bridge.
12. Live-action human in a stylized world stays photographic while the world stays hand-painted; perspective, light, shadow, contact, occlusion, atmosphere, and wind are shared.
13. Calm photobook albums prioritize behavior, sensory quiet, and narrative time over fashion posing.
14. Each image uses one main mechanism; album variety is axis variation, not background swapping.

## 5. Normalized chat timeline

This is a decision timeline reconstructed from the chat available to this build and from workspace artifacts. It is not presented as a verbatim transcript, and inaccessible conversations are not invented.

1. Semi-realistic Taoist material was proposed through a crane, Bagua structure, and yin-yang logic.
2. The first calibration target became a portrait comparable in visual authority to the successful Koi and Ink-Snow Serpent cases; the crane had to carry Taoist material rather than appear as a decorative bird.
3. When a generated image was not visible remotely, the required fallback became a complete prompt in chat or a directly viewable path.
4. Koi and Serpent were recovered as structural exemplars, not as subjects to repeat.
5. The semi-realistic couture family was expanded beyond animals to plants, artifacts, natural phenomena, and metaphysical carriers.
6. Blue-White Porcelain Gold-Seam was selected for development into metaphysical fashion with simplified symbols, depth of field, and coordinated three-dimensional, two-point-five-dimensional, and flat two-dimensional layers.
7. A successful portrait was expanded into a fashion album with materially varied, optically distinct shots rather than repeated backgrounds.
8. The user made Simplified Chinese an absolute requirement for every generated-image prompt.
9. The exact brand text T1 to 9 was added without rewriting the approved creative body; complete copy-ready prompt text became the delivery standard.
10. File-only delivery was rejected when prompt text was requested in chat.
11. The semantic output contract was reduced and locked to three sections: 母版锁, 分镜, 通用负面提示词. The brand rule stays inside 母版锁.
12. Shot names and section meanings were translated outside prompt blocks for Vietnamese review, without changing the Chinese generator text.
13. A new cross-medium case introduced one real adult Vietnamese woman in a loose white dress walking through a volumetric hand-painted animation world; reference logos and promotional text were ignored.
14. That case was expanded into a calm, relaxed photobook with unrestricted landscapes, behavior-led moments, and one coherent painted-world render contract.
15. The current request triggered a full source rebuild: audit the available source and chat decisions, remove duplication, preserve unique mechanisms, supply complete examples, and make the result portable across projects.

## 6. New universal addition

Law 14 was added:

Cross-medium images need both a render-boundary contract and a physical-bridge contract.

This rule emerged from the live-action painted-world case and generalizes to collage, diorama, porcelain couture, comic relief, and other mixed media.
