Validation before registration:

1. per-source context gates are approved or all conditions are satisfied;
2. all load-bearing dimensions have the correct authority owner;
3. every material test preregisters hypothesis, prediction, falsifier, fixed variables, causal bundle, planned samples, and acceptance rule;
4. all result IDs, settings, failures, denominator, and confounders are retained rather than only selected wins;
5. parent/control baselines and repeated samples are recorded when the family is a fusion;
6. at least three structurally different outputs pass, including close/anchor, environment/wide, and one hard mechanism;
7. a second subject or environment transfer and a difficult-condition/aspect stress test pass within declared scope;
8. at least one ablation or jurisdiction swap demonstrates a necessary mechanism/source/bridge;
9. one documented failure→repair establishes collapse, trigger, guard, and acceptance criterion;
10. identity, signature, relational invariants, and bounded variation survive across outputs;
11. essential motifs, translation, attribution, and authenticity language stay within context/permission;
12. no law is duplicated from core, and the family is registered in `library/13_FAMILY_REGISTRY.md` only after this evidence passes.
