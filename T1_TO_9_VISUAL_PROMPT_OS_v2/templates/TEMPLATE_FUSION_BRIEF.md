Reject, pause, or reframe the fusion if:

- any source is unknown/incomplete, conditionally unresolved, red/rejected, missing authority/provenance/rights/sensitivity basis, or falsely marked authentic;
- a load-bearing dimension has no owner, two silent owners, or an owner below the required authority;
- split-domain work has no domain grammar, boundary, or authored relationship; shared-world integration also requires global-physics authority and visible physical-bridge evidence;
- support sources compete on the same dimension without explicit resolution;
- an essential motif lacks function, context, permission, limits, or honest naming;
- the only source contribution is an unexplained or unauthorized motif/style label;
- emergent rules only restate borrowed motifs, labels, or colors;
- the selected bridge or non-integration relationship produces no observable/causal/formal difference, or ablation does not change the predicted effect;
- removing a support source leaves the intended effect unchanged;
- tension collapses into mud, a split image, or decorative conflict;
- signature composition, living-artist identity, or restricted cultural material is being imitated;
- evidence records only selected wins, omits the denominator/failures, or has no preregistered falsifier and acceptance rule;
- one attractive sample is the only evidence.
