# CINEMATIC VISUAL STORYTELLING

Formation knowledge. Not a family, not a LUT, not a delivery contract. Load on demand when the task is cinematic discussion, DNA extraction, or structural rebuild of a still. Current brief, current reference assignment, and latest correction outrank this book.

Working definition: a still can feel cinematic when character, event or state, viewpoint, space, light, and information are organized so the viewer can read an intended relation. This is a design tool, not a wall between photography and cinema.

Do not score cinematic by a fixed look: dark, brown, teal-orange, anamorphic, letterbox, grain, haze, or shallow DOF. Daylight, deep focus, static tableau, comedy, intimacy, animation, flat graphics, and structured impossibility can all be cinematic when the picture is organized.

Each chapter: mechanism → observable signs → how to choose → counterexample → turn into image description → failure/repair.

Sources used as design heuristics, not film-time laws:

- [F1] Gregg Toland on *Citizen Kane*: multiple sharp planes and staging can carry story in one frame. <https://theasc.com/article/realism-for-citizen-kane/>
- [F2] Hitchcock on method: meaning may sit in a hand–object relation and in the reaction, not only in a face. <https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/alfred-hitchcock-my-own-methods>
- Practitioner heuristic supplied by the user (author unknown outside the conversation): 角色轮廓决定景别，角色能力决定姿态，观看关系决定机位，空间层次决定清晰与遮挡.

Claims labeled `DESIGN_HEURISTIC` are not calibrated model measurements.

## 0. How to use this book

1. Observe before interpreting. If the image is missing, work from the prompt and mark `PROMPT_ONLY`.
2. Separate identity DNA from relational DNA. A visible trait is not automatically a lock.
3. Discuss when the user wants to discuss. Compile only after a decision or an explicit grant to choose.
4. A rebuild must change relation, action, space, or camera according to the brief. Recolor, grain, renaming a lens, or adding the word cinematic is not a rebuild.
5. One still has one moment. Do not write a dolly and a reverse shot into a single frame.

## 1. Intent and moment

**Mechanism.** The viewer should know or feel one thing from one representable instant. Before / during / after are design hypotheses, not a plot that must be illustrated.

**Signs.** A readable beat: a task underway, a pause, a meeting, a waiting, a formal rhythm. Contemplative stills do not need thriller stakes.

**Choose.** Name the residue after viewing. Pick one moment that can exist in a still. If the brief is presence or quiet, do not invent a crisis.

**Counterexample.** “Make it cinematic” by adding tears, running, or a secret chase when the brief is a quiet interior.

**Image description.** State who is doing what now, what just stopped, and what has not yet happened — only if those are visible.

**Failure / repair.** If the frame is a posed portrait with a plot caption, repair the beat (weight, hands, interrupted task), not the grade.

## 2. Performance and blocking

**Mechanism.** Character goal, what resists it, bodily capability, weight, body–head–eye directions, and hands on objects. Microgesture needs a cause. Neutral expression is not weak acting.

**Signs.** Support points; which foot or hand takes weight; a task that occupies the hands; head turning at a different rate from the torso.

**Choose.** Capability makes a gesture believable; occupation does not pick one pose. Start from the beat, then place the body.

**Counterexample.** Same standing center pose with a book pasted into the hands. That is a prop, not an interrupted task.

**Image description.** Weight, support, gaze target, and the one visible evidence of action.

**Failure / repair.** If the result is a beauty pose, repair action–support–gaze. Do not add LUT.

Practitioner reading of 角色能力决定姿态: capability constrains what the body can do; goal, moment, and bodily state choose the action. `DESIGN_HEURISTIC`.

## 3. Viewer relation

**Mechanism.** Does the viewer know more or less than the figure? Camera may observe, participate, take a POV, sit over a shoulder, be subjective, or be non-human. `unknown observer` is allowed. Do not invent a person behind the camera as fact.

**Signs.** Eye contact with the lens; a threshold between viewer and figure; a shoulder in the foreground; a reflection that sees what the body does not.

**Choose.** Viewing relation orients station. It does not always require a human observer inside the scene.

**Counterexample.** Looking at the lens is treated as proof of a second person or a POV shot. It only proves gaze direction.

**Image description.** Viewpoint type, observer identity (`unspecified` unless authored), known vs hidden information.

**Failure / repair.** If stealth/voyeurism was inferred from a railing, keep the threshold and drop the plot unless the user asked for it.

## 4. Shot size and silhouette

**Mechanism.** What must be readable decides crop and scale: identity, hands, prop, gesture, setting. Full body is not required because a costume is beautiful. Close-up is not automatically stronger than wide.

**Signs.** Whether the silhouette of the figure, the hand–object pair, or the room is the information that carries the beat.

**Choose.** Silhouette and required information help pick size; emotion may crop the silhouette away. `DESIGN_HEURISTIC` for 角色轮廓决定景别 — not a one-way chain.

**Counterexample.** Wide shot because “cinematic,” when the beat is a hand closing a book.

**Image description.** What must remain uncropped; what may fall away.

**Failure / repair.** If the event is off-frame, change crop or blocking, not sharpness.

## 5. Camera and perspective

**Mechanism.** Station, height, distance, orientation, and field-of-view *effect*. Describe visual consequence. Wide shot ≠ wide-angle. Distortion comes from distance and position. Do not infer exact focal length from an image without metadata.

**Signs.** Foreground compression or expansion; how much floor is seen; whether a near object occludes; whether the figure is seen from above, level, or below.

**Choose.** Viewing relation orients station; space and occlusion constrain it. Camera numbers in prose are appearance cues for GPT Image 2.5, not a physical simulation. [O4]

**Counterexample.** “85mm f/1.4” as proof of cinematic quality. High angle as proof of power or weakness.

**Image description.** Where the camera stands relative to architecture and body; what the FOV *does* to planes; do not state EXIF as fact.

**Failure / repair.** If the rebuild was supposed to keep a high-angle wood threshold and the prompt moved to eye level, that is an unlocked translation — reverse it unless the user opened the lock.

## 6. Space and attention

**Mechanism.** Planes, front/back, sightlines, occlusion, rest, selective or deep focus, light/contrast that lead the eye. Depth does not pick focus by itself. Focus is an optical and storytelling choice inside geometry. [F1] shows several sharp planes can share story. `DESIGN_HEURISTIC`.

**Signs.** A near structure that frames; a readable mid-ground event; a far plane that recedes without competing; a rest area of lower detail.

**Choose.** Spatial layers constrain occlusion and where sharpness can live; a focus plane is not born just because an object sits in the mid-ground. `DESIGN_HEURISTIC` for 空间层次决定清晰与遮挡.

**Counterexample.** Blur the whole background to “make it cinematic” when two distant events must both read (daylight/deep-focus brief).

**Image description.** Planes, what occludes what, what must stay visible, first read, rest areas.

**Failure / repair.** If frame-through wood is too dark to read the face, repair exposure/occlusion of the threshold, not by deleting the foreground.

## 7. Light and color

**Mechanism.** Visible or off-frame sources, direction, softness, fill, falloff, receivers, bounce, exposure, surface response. Motivated light may still be strongly staged. A practical in frame does not prove it is the only key. Color accents, separates, or rhythms when it helps structure.

**Signs.** Where the face or mechanism receives light; whether the near threshold is darker; whether a lamp is local or a global orange wash.

**Choose.** Light serves the beat and the first read. Do not default to teal-orange, haze, or low-key.

**Counterexample.** A daylight two-person still turned noir because “cinematic.”

**Image description.** World-space sources, what they hit, motivated exception if any.

**Failure / repair.** If the grade ate the event, restore source hierarchy. Do not add another LUT.

## 8. Mise-en-scène and outside-frame

**Mechanism.** Architecture, objects, emptiness, traces of action, looks that leave the frame. Environment has a function. Period costume or a film-still look is not a universal standard.

**Signs.** A table that explains an interrupted task; a door that creates a threshold; an empty chair that is a relation, not clutter.

**Choose.** Keep objects that prove the beat. Drop souvenir clutter.

**Counterexample.** Adding more antique props to “raise production value” until the first read splits.

**Image description.** Functional set pieces; what exists outside the frame only if it has a visible effect (light, gaze, occlusion).

**Failure / repair.** If the room is a warehouse of motifs, restore one functional set.

## 9. Still, series, and critique

**Mechanism.** One frame, one moment. A series varies blocking, station, beat, size, and relation while keeping the brief’s continuity.

**Signs.** For a still: no implied camera move that needs time. For a series: axes actually rotate.

**Choose.** If the user asked for one still, do not write a coverage sequence.

**Counterexample.** “Dolly in then reverse” inside one prompt block.

**Image description.** One beat. For series, name the axis that changes.

**Failure / repair.** Critique DNA kept vs lost; whether the new relation landed or collapsed to a portrait; whether occlusion hid evidence; whether an authored impossibility stayed in its licensed region. Repair the failed layer.

## 10. Practitioner four-clause — operational reading

Keep the sentence as a heuristic, not a one-way pipeline:

> 角色轮廓决定景别，角色能力决定姿态，观看关系决定机位，空间层次决定清晰与遮挡。

- Silhouette and required information help choose size; emotion may crop the silhouette.
- Capability makes gesture credible; goal, moment, and body state pick the action. A job title is not a pose.
- Viewing relation orients station; omniscient, abstract, and non-human cameras exist. The observer is not always a person in the scene.
- Spatial layers constrain occlusion and focus opportunity; a mid-ground object does not mint a focus plane.

You may start from space, from a gesture, or from an impossible relation, then check the loop.

## 11. Overclaims to refuse (regression)

These inferences are too strong. They must not re-enter analysis as facts:

- High angle does not by itself prove power or weakness; acting, scale, context, or an author decision is required.
- Looking at the lens proves gaze direction, not a second person and not a POV shot.
- A blocking foreground can suggest concealed observation; it does not prove surveillance or stealth.
- Key light, aperture, exact camera height, and exact focal length cannot be confirmed from a still without metadata.
- A beautiful face, a still pose, a vertical frame, or dense detail does not make an image un-cinematic.
- A large event is not required; waiting, presence, or formal rhythm can suffice.

## 12. Clean impossibility (scoped taste)

User taste, not a family: impossibility may be strong, but the main relation must read. Do not force photoreal on the whole image. Do not force every image into fantasy.

When the brief mixes media or an impossible mechanism, record:

- `first_read_owner`: person / mechanism / world, or an explained distributed pattern;
- which objects are real, which are flat graphic, which have a carrier (paper, glass, chopsticks);
- which laws hold, which law is broken on purpose, and the visible proof of the exception;
- boundary and crossing; do not add shadow/thickness that destroys intended flatness;
- attention hierarchy and rest; do not “clean” by always emptying the background.

Do not let a leftover face-first guard override an event-first or mechanism-first brief. Patch a conflicting family guard only with scope and evidence; do not rewrite every family.

Flat rendering is not “no depth.” Several flat silhouettes can stack, occlude, and change scale without becoming miniature 3D. `DESIGN_HEURISTIC`, distilled from the clean-impossibility draft; not a calibrated GPT Image 2.5 law.

## 13. GPT Image 2.5 — project rules, not model internals

`chat_host`, `target_generator`, and `bound_tool` are different fields. Writing a prompt *for* GPT Image 2.5 does not mean Grok is running that model.

If the user only says “GPT image 2.5,” Sunburst may be *recommended* for a complex reference job and must be labeled assumed/recommended. Keep the user’s named model if they named one.

A new prompt must be usable in another session: reference roles, final scene, invariants, and the change. No “keep as above, change that.”

Settings live in metadata/tool arguments. “4K, 85mm, f/1.4” in prose is not proof of resolution, lens, or DOF. Do not add seed/CFG/steps/image-weight from other families of models.

Do not send the whole JSON contract, citations, or the analysis chain to the renderer.

Rebuild may change several related factors as a new design. Debug changes one causal hypothesis. Do not use local-edit rules to block a layout rebuild the user asked for.

Reference-driven new scenes use original references by role. Edits use the designated target frame. Do not treat the latest output as identity authority unless assigned.

Exact identity is an inspection goal against the reference, not a pixel-identical promise.

Do not guess architecture, training data, or “the model reads the start twice.”

## 14. Failure table

| Failure | Cause | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Recolor called a rebuild | palette swapped, relation unchanged | require a relational/action/spatial delta |
| High angle = victim | psychology shortcut | describe station and acting, drop the power claim |
| Invented voyeur | railing + downward view | keep threshold; mark stealth as interpretation |
| Exact 85mm as fact | no EXIF | FOV effect only |
| Face-first ate the event | leftover portrait guard | restore first_read_owner from the brief |
| Flat graphic inflated to 3D | “depth” misread as volume | restore planar grammar and licensed crossing |
| Discussion emitted three prompts | skipped DISCUSS stop | stop after DNA + one decision question |
| Compile without a choice | silence treated as delegate | do not invent user_selected or delegated |
| Intentional reflection mismatch “fixed” | model-consistency habit | keep the authored exception; reject extra errors |
